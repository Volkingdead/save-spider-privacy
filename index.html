Save Spider Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-11

This privacy policy applies to the Save Spider mobile game for Android and iOS (the "Game"), made by Volkan Korkmaz (the "Developer", "we"). It explains what data the Game and its advertising partners use, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • The Game has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • Your scores, settings and progress are stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not receive this data.
  • The Game is free and shows ads. The ads are delivered by Unity LevelPlay and its ad networks, which use device data such as your device's advertising ID, as described below.
  • The Game asks for your year of birth once. Players under 16 never get personalised ads, and ads for players under 13 are child-directed.
  • You can limit ad personalisation through your consent choice, the iOS tracking prompt and your device settings.

Data stored on your device

The Game saves your high score, game settings, tutorial progress and your privacy choices on your device. This data never leaves your device through us. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

Advertising

The Game shows banner ads, interstitial ads, and an optional rewarded ad that you can choose to watch to continue a run. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay (formerly ironSource), an ad mediation platform, which selects an ad from these ad networks:

  • ironSource Ads (Unity)
  • Unity Ads (Unity)

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, these partners collect data from your device directly. This can include:

  • your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS when you allow tracking) and other device identifiers such as the IDFV on iOS or the App Set ID on Android;
  • your IP address and the approximate location (country or city) derived from it;
  • device information such as model, operating system, language, screen size and network type;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, clicked or watched.

Depending on your choices below, the advertising ID may be used to show ads that are personalised to your interests. If you do not allow this, you still see ads, but they are less personalised. These partners act under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy (covers Unity Ads, ironSource Ads and Unity LevelPlay)
  • Unity Cookie and Advertising Policy

Your age

The first time the Game starts, it asks for your year of birth. Only the year is stored, on your device, and it is never sent to us. It is used only to choose how ads are shown:

  • Under 13: ads are marked as directed at children. Only ad networks certified for Google Play's Families program are used, your device's advertising ID is not used, and ads are not personalised. The Game does not show the consent or tracking prompts.
  • 13 to 15: ads are not personalised, your data is not sold or shared for personalised advertising, and the Game does not show the consent or tracking prompts.
  • 16 and over: the choices described below apply.

Your consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are 16 or over and in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before our ad partners may use your data for personalised ads. If you accept, your consent is the legal basis for that processing. If you decline, you still get ads, but they are not personalised.

You can change your choice at any time from Privacy on the Game's start screen. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew it.

iOS App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, if you are 16 or over, the Game asks for your permission before it can access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared with our ad partners, and the ads you see are less personalised. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

How to limit ad personalisation

  • Android: open Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads on some devices) and delete or reset your advertising ID.
  • iOS: open Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and turn off tracking for the Game.
  • In the Game (EEA, UK, Switzerland): use Privacy on the start screen.

Uninstalling the Game stops all further data collection by the Game and its ad partners.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not hold personal data about you on our own systems: everything the Game itself stores stays on your device. The data used for advertising is held by our ad partners, so requests about that data are best sent to them through the Unity privacy policy linked above. You can also contact us (see Contact) and we will help you.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

We do not sell your personal information for money. Our ad partners' use of device identifiers for personalised advertising may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under California law. You can opt out by resetting or deleting your advertising ID or turning off tracking as described above. You will not be treated differently for exercising your rights.

International transfers

Our ad partners may process data in countries outside your own, including the United States. They are responsible for using appropriate safeguards for such transfers, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Data retention

Data stored on your device is kept until you uninstall the Game. Data collected by our ad partners is kept according to their own privacy policies.

Children

The Game can be played by everyone, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children: the Game itself stores nothing outside the device, and for players under 13 ads are child-directed, without the advertising ID or personalisation, as described in "Your age". Players under 16 never get personalised ads. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions about your child's data, please contact us.

Security

The Game does not send your data to us. Our ad partners use their own technical and organisational measures to protect the data they collect.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add or change an ad partner. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. If a change affects the consent you gave, the Game will ask for your choice again.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your privacy, open an issue at github.com/Volkingdead/save-spider-privacy/issues. Issues are public, so please do not include personal data in them.
